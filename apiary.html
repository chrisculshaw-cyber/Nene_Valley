Our Bees

A field full of busy neighbours

Tucked at the top of the lavender field sits our apiary — a cluster of colourful hives, each hand-painted and cared for through the seasons. The bees forage the lavender on our doorstep and the wildflowers and hedgerows all around, which is what gives our honey its gentle, floral character.

Beekeeping is slow, careful work: checking the colonies, keeping them healthy and strong, and taking only the honey the bees can spare. It’s a labour of love, and one we’re always happy to share with visitors.

Meet the Beekeepers

The two of us behind the hives

Every hive here is looked after by hand, by the two of us. We’re Chris and Daren, both beekeepers, and between us we do the weekly inspections, the swarm control, the feeding and treating, and the careful work of getting each colony through winter strong and healthy — taking off only the honey the bees can spare.

The field is Daren’s too. He owns the land the apiary sits on, grows the lavender the bees can’t get enough of, and distils it into the essential oils at the heart of our Nene Valley Lavender products. So when our bees work the lavender rows each summer, they’re foraging on flowers we’ve planted and tended ourselves — which is what gives our honey its gentle, floral note.
