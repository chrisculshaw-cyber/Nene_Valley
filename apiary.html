Our Bees

A field full of busy neighbours

Tucked at the top of the lavender field sits our apiary — a cluster of colourful hives, each hand-painted and cared for through the seasons. The bees forage the lavender on our doorstep and the wildflowers and hedgerows all around, which is what gives our honey its gentle, floral character.

Beekeeping is slow, careful work: checking the colonies, keeping them healthy and strong, and taking only the honey the bees can spare. It’s a labour of love, and one we’re always happy to share with visitors.

Sponsor a Hive

Give a hive a home & a name

Sponsoring a hive is a lovely way to support local beekeeping — whether for yourself, as a gift, or in memory of someone. Your hive gets its own named slate plaque out in the apiary, and you’re welcome to come and meet your bees.

  1. 🌻

    Choose your hive

    Pick a hive to sponsor and give it a name — your family name, a loved one, or something playful. It’s entirely yours.

  2. 🏷️

    We add your plaque

    We fit a smart slate plaque to your hive with its name and your dedication, out in the apiary for all to see.

  3. 🍯

    Come and meet your bees

    Visit your hive through the season, see how the colony is doing, and share in a little of the honey they make.

Hive Visits

Suit up and say hello

There’s nothing quite like standing beside a working hive. We kit you out in a bee suit and take you up to the apiary to see the colony up close — calm, careful, and endlessly fascinating. It’s a favourite with children and grown-ups alike.
