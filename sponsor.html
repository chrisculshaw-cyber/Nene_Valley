Sponsor a Hive

Help one of our colonies through its year — and see your name on its plaque.

How it works

Sponsor a hive for 12 months

A sponsorship doesn’t buy a hive — the bees stay in our care. What it does is fund a full year of looking after one colony, so it comes through winter healthy and strong. In return, the hive carries your name for the year.

Your 12-month sponsorship helps us to
  • Feed the colony when forage runs short in autumn and early spring
  • Get them through winter — the hardest months for any colony
  • Treat them against varroa mite and keep disease at bay
  • Keep the colony strong with regular inspections through the season
  1. 🌻

    Sponsor a hive for a year

    Choose a name for it — your family, a loved one, a business, or something playful. Lovely as a gift or in memory of someone, too.

  2. 🏷️

    We add your plaque

    A smart slate plaque goes on the hive with its name and your dedication, out in the apiary for the 12 months of your sponsorship.

  3. 🐝

    Follow the colony’s year

    Come and see the hive you’re supporting and how its colony is getting on through the seasons.

Sponsorships run for 12 months and can be renewed each year.
